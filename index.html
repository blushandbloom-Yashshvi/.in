Our Story

Blush & Bloom

Blush & Bloom by Yashshvi was created with one simple dream — to make every gift feel special, meaningful, and unforgettable. Founded by Yashshvi, the brand is built with passion, creativity, and love for gifting. Every bouquet, jewellery piece, and hamper is thoughtfully curated to turn your special moments into beautiful memories.

From birthdays and anniversaries to surprises and celebrations, we believe every gift should tell a story — which is why we offer beautifully crafted gifts that can be customized just the way you imagine. We proudly deliver happiness across India, ensuring quality, elegance, and customer satisfaction in every order. Our mission is simple: to create gifts that bring smiles, strengthen relationships, and make every occasion truly unforgettable.

Shipping Policy

At Blush & Bloom, we strive to deliver your orders safely and as quickly as possible.

Order Processing

Once your order is confirmed, we begin preparing it for dispatch. You can track your order anytime by visiting My Account → My Orders on our website.

Estimated Delivery Time

  • Within Mumbai: Delivery is usually completed within 5 days.
  • Outside Mumbai: Delivery may take up to 10 days, although many orders may arrive earlier depending on the courier service and location.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are calculated and collected after your order is placed. This is because the final delivery cost depends on:

  • The delivery distance.
  • The quantity, size, and weight of the products ordered.

We follow this method to ensure that you pay only the actual delivery charges and not a fixed or higher shipping fee. Our goal is to keep your shipping cost as fair and affordable as possible.

Order Tracking

You can track your order at any time through My Account → My Orders.

Delivery Delays

Delivery timelines may be affected due to weather conditions, festivals, public holidays, courier service delays, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Incorrect Address

Please ensure that your shipping address and contact details are correct while placing your order. Any delay or additional charges caused by an incorrect or incomplete address will be the responsibility of the customer.

Replacement Policy

At Blush & Bloom, we carefully inspect every product before shipping. However, if you receive a damaged or defective item, we are happy to help.

Replacement Policy

  • If the product delivered to you is found to be defective or damaged, we will provide a free replacement of the same product.
  • No refund will be issued for delivered orders. Instead, an eligible replacement will be sent at no extra cost.

Eligibility for Replacement

To be eligible for a replacement:

  1. You must record a continuous unboxing video from the moment the package is opened.
  2. The defect or damage must be clearly visible in the video.
  3. Claims made without a valid unboxing video may not be accepted.

Our team will review your claim, and if approved, the replacement product will be shipped to you free of charge.
